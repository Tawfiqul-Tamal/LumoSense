# 🌗 LumoSense: Light and Darkness Detection System

## Overview

**LumoSense** is a hardware-based electronics project designed to detect ambient light and darkness in real-time. The system utilizes a **Light Dependent Resistor (LDR)** to monitor surrounding light intensity and automatically controls LED indicators based on environmental conditions.

A **BC547 NPN BJT transistor** is used as a switching device to drive the LED outputs efficiently. When the LDR senses low light or darkness, the transistor activates the corresponding LEDs, providing an automatic indication of the detected condition.

This project demonstrates the practical implementation of sensor-based circuits, transistor switching mechanisms, and real-time environmental monitoring systems.

## Working Principle

1. The **LDR** continuously monitors ambient light intensity.
2. Its resistance changes according to the amount of light falling on its surface.
3. The resulting voltage variation is supplied to the **BC547 transistor**.
4. The transistor operates as an electronic switch.
5. When darkness is detected, the transistor turns ON and activates the designated LED.
6. When sufficient light is present, the circuit switches to the alternative LED indication.
